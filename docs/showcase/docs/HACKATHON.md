# WhiteBox AI: National Founder Cup and version record

**National Founder Cup 2026** is the name used by the organizer; the team also refers
to it as National Founders Cup. The team reports participation without a prize and
has paused development.

[AI Startify's organizer page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ai-startify) contains its
announcement of the Black Swan partnership and the Almaty competition, with an August 1
final. That event context does not establish WhiteBox's placement or finalist status.
No such placement is claimed here.
